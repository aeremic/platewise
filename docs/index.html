Platewise

Your food diary, colored by how healthy each day was.

Log what you eat in a few taps and see every day on a calendar colored green, orange or red. No account, no ads, no tracking — your diary stays on your phone.

Help & FAQ

How is a day’s color decided?

Each food has a color (healthy, moderate or unhealthy). The day’s color is the average of the foods you ate, weighted by how much you ate, so a big unhealthy portion counts more than a small one. Your daily goals then adjust it: reaching your fiber goal makes the day one step better, and going over your calorie or sugar limit means it can’t be green (more than 25% over makes it red). Open a day to see the reasons.

Can I change the foods and their colors?

Yes. Settings → Categories lets you add, rename, recolor or remove categories and set the portion size, calories, fiber and sugar for one portion. Built-in values are approximate.

How do I move my diary to a new phone?

On the old phone open Settings → Backup → Export and save the file (Files, Google Drive, email or AirDrop). On the new phone install Platewise, open Settings → Backup → Import and pick the file. This works between iPhone and Android. Importing replaces the data on the new phone, and you can undo it.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. See the privacy policy for details.

Is this medical advice?

No. Platewise is for everyday awareness. Nutrition values and colors are estimates; talk to a doctor or registered dietitian about your diet.
