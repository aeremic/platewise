### Description (≤ 4000)
Platewise is a simple, private food diary that shows how healthy you eat — at a glance.

Log what you ate in a few taps and every day on your calendar gets a color: green for a healthy day, orange for a mixed one, red for a day of treats. Over weeks, the calendar tells the story without spreadsheets or endless numbers.

LOG IN SECONDS
• Tap foods from ready-made categories like salad, fish, pasta, pizza or ice cream
• Your most recent foods are always one tap away
• Adjust portions with a stepper (½, 1, 1½, 2…) or type exact values
• Add your own categories with an emoji, a color and nutrition per portion

A COLOR THAT MEANS SOMETHING
• Each day is rated by what you ate and how much — a big portion of something unhealthy counts more than a small one
• Daily goals for calories, fiber and sugar adjust the color: reach your fiber goal and the day gets better, go over your sugar limit and it can’t be green
• Open any day to see why it got its color, with a clear breakdown and goal progress
• The preview updates live while you add food, before you save

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Everything is stored only on your phone
• Export a backup file anytime and import it on another phone

HANDY EXTRAS
• Ask AI opens Google AI Mode to look up a portion’s calories, fiber and sugar
• Dark design with Liquid Glass
• Works offline

Platewise is for everyday awareness. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Nutrition values and day colors are estimates; consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
